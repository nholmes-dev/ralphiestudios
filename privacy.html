Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 September 2026

Ralphie Studios ("we", "us", "our") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights under the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR).

Who We Are

Ralphie Studios is an independent development studio based in the United Kingdom, creating games, apps and digital sports experiences. This website operates at ralphiestudios.com. For any privacy-related queries, contact us at: contact@ralphiestudios.com

What Information We Collect

We collect only the personal data needed to operate the website and respond to enquiries:

  • Contact enquiries: If you email us directly, we receive your email address and the content of your message.
  • Server logs: Our hosting provider (Cloudflare) may log basic technical data such as your IP address, browser type, and pages visited. These logs are used for security and performance purposes. See Cloudflare's privacy policy for details.

There is currently no active Wheelchair Rugby League email waitlist form on this website. We do not use tracking cookies, analytics scripts, or advertising networks on this website.

How We Use Your Information

  • Contact enquiries are used solely to respond to your message.
  • Server-log information may be used for website security, reliability and performance.

Legal Basis for Processing

  • Contact enquiries: processed on the basis of legitimate interests (responding to your message).
  • Security and server logs: processed where necessary for our legitimate interests in maintaining the security and reliability of the website.

Data Retention

Email correspondence is retained only as long as necessary to fulfil the purpose for which it was received. Server logs are retained in accordance with Cloudflare's standard retention periods.

Your Rights

Under UK GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request erasure of your data
  • Object to or restrict our processing of your data
  • Lodge a complaint with the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at contact@ralphiestudios.com.

Third-Party Links

This website may link to external sites (GitHub, X/Twitter, etc.). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites and encourage you to review their policies.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The "last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
