The Studio

Small studio.
Serious ideas.

Ralphie Studios is an independent development studio creating games, apps and digital experiences with a particular love for sport. Projects are built from the ground up with care — from gameplay mechanics and physics to UI, presentation and the small details that make something feel authentic.

The studio is currently developing the first Wheelchair Rugby League video game in Unreal Engine 5. BaizeBoard — a live digital snooker scoreboard — is already available on Android and Web.

BaizeBoard

Digital scoring built for the baize.

A modern digital snooker scoreboard for players, clubs and match nights. BaizeBoard provides a clean scoring experience with full stat tracking — frames, breaks, fouls and match history — across web and Android, without needing traditional scoreboard hardware.
